### About me

I'm a AI & Full Stack & Automation Engineer with **professional software development experience**, including **4+ years building and deploying AI-powered applications and machine-learning systems**.
My work combines **AI engineering**, **backend architecture**, and **full-stack development**. I build systems that connect machine-learning models with scalable APIs, data pipelines, and user-facing applications—from conversational analytics and document intelligence to computer vision and neural speech synthesis.

---

### What I'm working on

- 🤖 **Generative AI & language systems** — integrating LLMs into applications using OpenAI APIs, Hugging Face, LangChain, and LlamaIndex, with RAG, embeddings, and vector search for contextual retrieval.
- 👁️ **Computer vision & document intelligence** — building GPU-accelerated image, video, and face-processing pipelines, alongside OCR services and automated document-ingestion workflows.
- 🎙️ **Speech AI & model deployment** — developing multilingual text-to-speech APIs with StyleTTS2 and CoquiTTS, and optimizing models for efficient GPU inference.
- ⚙️ **Backend architecture & distributed systems** — designing Python and FastAPI services, REST and GraphQL APIs, microservices, and event-driven workflows with an emphasis on throughput, reliability, and maintainability.
- 🌐 **Full-stack application development** — connecting AI capabilities to practical interfaces using React, Next.js, Angular, TypeScript, and Node.js.
- ☁️ **Cloud infrastructure & MLOps** — deploying containerized services with Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, and GCP; automating infrastructure and releases with Terraform and GitHub Actions; and tracking experiments with MLflow.
- 📊 **Data engineering & performance** — building ETL pipelines and retrieval systems with SQL/NoSQL databases, Kafka, FAISS, Pinecone, and Weaviate, while improving processing speed, inference latency, and infrastructure costs.
